ChatGPT for Swiss Companies: First Tools and Workflows That Pay Off

AI AgentsAutomationStrategy

ChatGPT and similar AI tools are no longer a distant dream for Swiss companies — but choosing the right tools and introducing sensible workflows is often easier said than done. Many businesses do not know where to start, and shy away from the cost or the effort of a large-scale rollout.

Yet an AI introduction does not have to be expensive or complex. There is a range of tools and approaches that suit the Swiss mid-market and deliver first wins on a small budget.

The right tools to start with

To begin with, the tools that pay off fastest are the ones you can put to work quickly and that bring immediate relief in daily business:

  • ChatGPT Plus: the base for many applications, from drafting text to generating ideas.
  • Claude, from Anthropic: particularly good for longer documents and nuanced conversations.
  • Image AI such as Midjourney or DALL-E: for simple visuals and illustrations without an in-house design team.
  • Automation tools such as Make or Zapier: to simplify recurring work such as filtering email or entering data.

Practical workflows that pay off immediately

The key to success is not automating every task, but targeting the places where real value is created. Here are some proven workflows:

An email routine

Use AI to summarise incoming messages and draft first replies. In practice:

  1. Forward emails automatically to a summarising service.
  2. Let the AI draft a short reply in your tone.
  3. Review the draft, correct it and send it.

Collecting and developing ideas

When you are staring at a blank page, AI can serve as a springboard:

  1. Enter a short goal or problem.
  2. Let the AI sketch five different approaches.
  3. Pick the best option and build it out.

Important boundaries

If you work with sensitive data — in law, medicine or finance, for example — never paste confidential details into public consumer tools until you have verified that the setup complies with the Swiss Data Protection Act. Remove identifying details first, or use an approved configuration. Even in less regulated work, the final message should always be reviewed by a human before it goes out.

The goal is simple: faster access to useful AI tools, clearer workflows — and less time spent on admin, more time for what really matters.
