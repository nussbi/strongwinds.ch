How Swiss Solopreneurs Can Turn WhatsApp and Email Inquiries into a Simple CRM

Sales CRM Admin

A typical Swiss solopreneur gets inquiries from everywhere: a website form, an email, a WhatsApp message, maybe even a voice note after a client meeting. The problem is not demand. The problem is that the next step often lives in your head instead of in a system.

That is how opportunities slip away. One message gets answered immediately, another stays buried in your inbox, and a promising prospect hears back two days too late. You do not necessarily need a heavyweight CRM to fix this. Often, you just need one simple capture workflow and a little AI support.

The messy lead problem

When inquiries arrive across three or four channels, the same small problems repeat every week:

  • No single overview: You cannot see all open conversations in one place.
  • Slow follow-up: You know what to reply, but writing every answer takes longer than it should.
  • Missed next steps: A prospect asked for pricing, a call, or a document — and the task disappears under daily work.

A simple CRM without buying a CRM

Start with a lightweight structure. One spreadsheet, Notion table, or even a simple text file can be enough in the beginning. The goal is to capture every new inquiry in one place with five fields: name, source, summary, next step, and follow-up date.

Then create three clear stages: New, Waiting, and Closed. That alone already removes a surprising amount of mental load.

Where AI actually helps

AI does not need to replace your customer relationship management. It just needs to reduce the repetitive work around it.

  • Summarize inquiries: Turn a long WhatsApp thread or email exchange into a three-line brief.
  • Draft replies: Ask for a polite same-day response in your tone, with a clear next step.
  • Extract actions: Pull out deadlines, promised documents, callback dates, or quote requests.
“For most small businesses, the real win is not a more complicated tool. It is a cleaner daily habit.”

A 15-minute daily routine

A practical workflow looks like this:

  1. Move every new inquiry into your capture list before lunch.
  2. Use AI to generate a short summary and a first draft reply.
  3. Review the draft yourself, adjust the tone, and send it.
  4. Write down the next action and the date you want to follow up.

In practice, this means fewer forgotten leads and much faster response times without turning your business into an enterprise software project.

Important guardrails

If you work with sensitive data — for example in medicine, law, or finance — do not paste confidential details into public consumer tools without thinking. Remove identifying details first or use an approved setup. Even in less regulated work, the final message should always be reviewed by a human before it goes out.

The goal is simple: fewer dropped conversations, clearer follow-ups, and a small system you will actually keep using next month.
